About EduFocus_

What is
EduFocus?

EduFocus is an interactive learning platform designed to transform traditional PDF lessons into shorter and more engaging learning materials.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Long lessons.
Wandering minds.

Some students have difficulty staying focused during long lessons or while reading lengthy study materials.

02 / OUR SOLUTION

Less at once.
More that sticks.

EduFocus allows students to upload PDF lessons and turn them into:

  • Visual summaries
  • Flashcards
  • Short reading materials
  • Interactive quizzes

The platform also includes focus and study tools such as Pomodoro sessions, progress tracking, and rewards.

03 / OUR GOAL

Make learning
feel manageable.

Help students study in shorter, manageable sessions while making learning more interactive.

Contact

EduFocus is a student study project. For questions, feedback, or help with your account, contact the project team through your class or school’s project channel.

YOUR STUDY SPACE

Privacy

Your account details identify your study space. Uploaded lessons are accessible through your signed-in account, and you can delete them from your library. Timer settings are saved in this browser; completed focus sessions, study streaks, and PDF pages you view are saved to your account. The current version does not send PDFs to an external AI service.
